# Phase 1 – Brainstorming & Ideation

## Project Name
EduGenie – Google Gemini Powered Learning Assistant

## Problem Identification

Students often face difficulties while understanding complex academic
concepts, finding quick explanations, preparing practice questions, and
summarizing lengthy study materials.

Traditional learning methods may not always provide personalized and
instant assistance to every student.

## Initial Idea

The initial idea was to develop an AI-powered educational assistant that
could understand student questions and provide useful learning support.

## Brainstorming Ideas

- AI-based Question Answering
- Concept Explanation
- Automatic Quiz Generation
- Text Summarization
- Personalized Learning Recommendations
- AI-powered Study Assistant

## Final Idea

The above ideas were combined to develop EduGenie, an AI-powered learning
assistant using Google Gemini.

## Main Objective

To develop an intelligent learning assistant that helps students
understand concepts, practice questions, summarize content, and receive
learning recommendations.

## Expected Outcome

EduGenie should provide students with a simple platform for
AI-assisted learning.
