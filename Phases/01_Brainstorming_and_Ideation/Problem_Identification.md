# Problem Identification

## Problem

Students may spend considerable time searching for explanations,
practice questions, and summaries from different sources.

## Major Problems

1. Difficulty understanding complex concepts.
2. Lack of instant academic assistance.
3. Time-consuming manual quiz preparation.
4. Difficulty summarizing lengthy content.
5. Lack of personalized learning guidance.

## Proposed Solution

EduGenie provides AI-powered educational assistance through Google Gemini.
